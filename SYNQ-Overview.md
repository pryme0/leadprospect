> **SYNQ finds people who are *already* asking for what you sell — across LinkedIn, TikTok, and Instagram — qualifies them with AI, hands you their contact details, and lets you reach out and close, all from one workspace.**

It turns social noise into a pipeline of ready-to-buy leads.

## The problem it solves

Most businesses burn money on cold outreach — messaging people who never asked. SYNQ flips that model. It listens to public social conversations in real time and surfaces **buyer-intent signals** — the moment someone posts:

- *"Just moved back to Nigeria and need a house."*
- *"Frustrated with the lack of transparency in real estate."*
- *"Looking for eco-friendly housing options."*

Those are warm prospects raising their hand. SYNQ catches them the instant they do — so your team spends time only on people with real, demonstrated intent.

## How SYNQ grows your business & generates revenue

A closed-loop pipeline that refreshes itself every day:

| Stage | What happens | Why it makes you money |
|-------|--------------|------------------------|
| **1. Listen** | Crawls LinkedIn, TikTok & Instagram for the pain points and buying phrases your customers use | You reach prospects *before* your competitors even know they exist |
| **2. Qualify (AI)** | Every post is AI-scored into **HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW intent**; noise and marketing posts are filtered out | Your team never wastes time on tyre-kickers — only real prospects |
| **3. Enrich** | Each qualified lead gets a **verified email + phone** appended automatically | You can actually reach them — no dead ends |
| **4. Act** | Leads flow into your **Lead Queue → Pipeline → Outreach** to message, sequence, and track to close | Faster follow-up = higher reply rates = more closed deals |
| **5. Sync** | Everything pushes to your **CRM** so your existing process isn't disrupted | Your sales motion stays intact; SYNQ just fuels it |

**Bottom line:** instead of paying for stale lists and chasing cold contacts, you get a compounding pipeline of high-intent leads — higher conversion, shorter sales cycles, lower cost per acquisition.

## All your handles in one platform

The **Integrations hub** connects your entire stack through a secure OAuth framework with **encrypted token storage**. Connect once — SYNQ works everywhere:

- **Social sources** — LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram *(the lead-generation engines)*

One login. One workspace. No more juggling ten browser tabs and disconnected tools.
